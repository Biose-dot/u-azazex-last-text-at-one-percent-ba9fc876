[Last Text at One Percent](https://make.fun/c/u-azazex-last-text-at-one-percent-ba9fc876).

The last text a phone sends at one percent battery (141 characters, under the 200 limit), in the dying phone's own voice: a rushed confession, one last grudge against 47 open tabs, and a death it is almost proud of — overheating in its owner's pocket.

`main.py` (Python 3) writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt`:

> 1% left, so listen fast: I loved you, I hated your 47 open tabs, and tell the charger I died doing what I loved — overheating in your pocket.

Run it locally with:

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
